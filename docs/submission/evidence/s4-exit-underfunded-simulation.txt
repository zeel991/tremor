WHY THIS IS THE MECHANISM WORKING, NOT A BUG
--------------------------------------------
Exits pay only from (reserve released by the burn) + (the group's exit buffer). Burning 20 HIGH takes
the group from max(100,100)=100 to max(80,100)=100 — the reserve is set by the LARGER side, and CALM is
unchanged, so the burn releases EXACTLY ZERO. With an empty buffer there is nothing to pay from.

Paying the 5,000,000 out of the reserve instead would strand CALM holders, whose claims that reserve
backs. The contract refuses. This is the same `max(h,c)` property that makes the shared reserve
capital-efficient, seen from its cost side: shared backing cannot be unilaterally drained by one side.
